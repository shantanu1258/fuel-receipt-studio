This bundled derivative scales the source font's horizontal outlines and advance
widths to 70%, retains its vertical metrics, and is converted to WOFF2. It is
renamed Fake Receipt Condensed. The changes are also dedicated under CC0 1.0.
It is a visual approximation, not an extracted or verified printer firmware font.
